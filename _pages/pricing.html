One number on your invoice. We don't itemize the costs underneath — the rate you see is the rate you pay.

Are there listing fees or subscriptions?

No. Listing is free. The Ezley fee applies only to completed transactions.

When does the Ezley fee come out of my payout?

At payout time. Buyer pays the gross; we deduct the bundled Ezley fee before the funds settle to your account. You see one line, one number.

Ready to list?
