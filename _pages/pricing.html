What's included

How is my seller fee calculated?

By the size of your sale — nothing else. The rate comes from the sale price alone, not the payment method and not your verification tier. The four bands above cover every sale.

Are there listing fees or subscriptions?

No. Listing is free. The Ezley fee applies only to completed transactions.

When does the Ezley fee come out of my payout?

At payout time. The buyer pays the sale price; we deduct your Ezley fee before the funds settle to your account. You see one line, one number.
