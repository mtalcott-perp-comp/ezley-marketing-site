Who's behind Ezley

Trust starts with knowing who you're trusting. Here's who's building Ezley, and how to verify it for yourself.

Founder

Marc Talcott

I've spent twenty-five years building software, most of it in finance, retail, and integration architecture. Today I'm Director of Software Engineering at Inmar Intelligence, where I lead teams designing event-driven, AI-enabled financial systems. Before that, a long run of senior engineering and engineering-management roles at the same intersection of money, events, and scale.

My technical home base is event sourcing and event-driven architecture — systems where every action is recorded as an immutable event, so the history of what happened is the source of truth. That's the same backbone Ezley runs on. For a marketplace where buyers wire real money to people they've never met, "we have a perfect audit trail" isn't a marketing line; it's an architectural decision made on day one.

I have a Computer Science degree from UNC Greensboro and was inducted into Upsilon Pi Epsilon, the computing honor society. I live near Greensboro, North Carolina, where my spouse and I also run Talcott Farm & Nursery, a small organic operation. Ezley is the project I've wanted to build for years: the trust layer that high-value peer-to-peer commerce has always needed and never quite had.

Why this page exists

Most marketplaces hide their operators behind a logo and a customer-service email. That works fine when transactions are small and the platform's guarantees are limited to a refund button.

Ezley is built for transactions where that isn't enough — tractors, used vehicles, professional cameras, real furniture. Money in serious amounts moves between people who have never met. The product is the trust layer in between. And trust starts, before any of the mechanics, with knowing the name of the person and the company on the other side of it.

So that's what this page is. Now you know.
