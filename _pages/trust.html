Identity verification

Every seller and buyer has a verification tier visible on their profile. Higher tiers unlock larger transactions and faster payouts.

BASIC

Email verified. New accounts start here.

VERIFIED

Email + phone + government ID. Required to sell above per-category thresholds.

TRUSTED_SELLER

Email + phone + government ID + address + transaction history. Auto-approved listings, faster payout.

Disputes

Disputes follow a defined process. If you and the other party can't resolve a problem directly, you can open a dispute on the transaction.

A named Ezley operator handles each case — not a queue. The adjudication framework is documented, the steps are the same for every case, and either party has the right to escalate.

Trust is the product.

Everything else — escrow, identity, dispute resolution, concierge — is what that means in practice.
